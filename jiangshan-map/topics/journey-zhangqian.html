張騫鑿空西域行跡（前138年 — 前126年 · 西漢建元三年至元朔三年）

Zhang Qian's Mission to the Western Regions (138 BCE – 126 BCE · Western Han)

張騫鑿空西域行跡 · 逐站時空座標、古今地名與史料詩詞詳解（9 站）

第 1 站 長安（今陝西西安）· 奉詔募使·發自長安 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Imperial Commission at Chang'an
前138年（建元三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

漢武帝欲聯合大月氏共擊匈奴，張騫以郎應募，與堂邑氏胡奴甘父率百餘人自長安出發西行。

Emperor Wu of Han sought an alliance with the Great Yuezhi against the Xiongnu; Zhang Qian volunteered and departed Chang'an with over a hundred men.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“騫以郎應募，使月氏，與堂邑氏胡奴甘父俱出隴西。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Zhang Qian volunteered as a palace attendant to go as envoy to the Yuezhi, departing Longxi with Ganfu."
第 2 站 隴西（今甘肅臨洮）· 出隴西塞·踏入絕域 Longxi (Lintao, Gansu) — Crossing the Longxi Frontier
前138年（建元三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

自隴西郡出塞西渡黃河，踏入匈奴右賢王與渾邪王控扼的河西之地，由此西去萬裡皆屬未知絕域。

Crossing the Han frontier at Longxi Commandery into the Xiongnu-held Hexi Corridor toward the uncharted Western Regions.

《漢書·張騫傳》：“出隴西，徑匈奴，匈奴得之。”Book of Han: "Departing Longxi, he passed through Xiongnu territory and was captured."
第 3 站 姑臧·河西（今甘肅武威）· 羈留匈奴·持節十載 Guzang (Hexi) (Wuwei, Gansu) — Detained by the Xiongnu for a Decade
前138年（建元三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

途經河西走廊為匈奴騎兵截獲，押送單於王庭。單於予妻生子，然張騫始終“持漢節不失”，羈留十餘載待機西奔。

Captured in the Hexi Corridor and held for over ten years by the Xiongnu Chanyu, yet Zhang Qian never relinquished his Han imperial tally.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“留騫十餘歲，予妻，有子，然騫持漢節不失。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Detained over ten years and given a wife and son, yet Zhang Qian kept his Han imperial staff unblemished."
第 4 站 焉耆·北道（今新疆焉耆）· 乘隙脫走·疾馳西域北道 Yanqi (Karasahr) (Yanqi, Xinjiang) — Escape Along the Northern Tarim Oasis Route
前129年（元光六年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

匈奴監管漸寬，張騫與甘父乘隙逃脫，循天山南麓綠洲晝夜西馳數十日，穿越車師、焉耆、龜茲諸國。

Seizing a moment of lax guard, Zhang Qian and Ganfu escaped westward for dozens of days along the northern Tarim oases at the foot of the Tian Shan.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“居匈奴中，益寬，騫因與其屬亡鄉月氏，西走數十日。”Records of the Grand Historian: "When surveillance eased, Zhang Qian fled with his men toward the Yuezhi, riding west for dozens of days."
第 5 站 大宛·貳師城（今費爾幹納盆地）· 越蔥嶺抵大宛·得發導譯 Dayuan (Fergana) (Fergana Valley) — Crossing the Pamirs to Dayuan (Fergana)
前129年（元光六年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

翻越帕米爾高原（蔥嶺）抵達盛產汗血寶馬的大宛國。大宛王久聞漢朝饒財欲通而不得，見張騫大喜，特派嚮導譯員護送赴康居。

Crossing the Pamir Mountains into Fergana (Dayuan), whose king welcomed Zhang Qian warmly and provided guides and interpreters.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“大宛聞漢之饒財，欲通不得，見騫，喜……為發導驛抵康居。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Dayuan had heard of Han wealth and rejoiced at meeting Zhang Qian, dispatching post-guides to escort him to Kangju."
第 6 站 康居（今錫爾河中游）· 經康居傳致大月氏 Kangju (Sogdiana) (Tashkent / Samarkand region) — Relay Through Kangju to the Oxus
前129年（元光六年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

由大宛導驛護送至康居國，再由康居王庭遣騎護送南下媯水（阿姆河）流域的大月氏王庭。

Escorted through Kangju between the Syr Darya and Zeravshan rivers, and relayed southward toward the Amu Darya (Oxus).

《史記·大宛列傳》：“抵康居，康居傳致大月氏。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Reaching Kangju, he was relayed onward to the Great Yuezhi."
第 7 站 大月氏·藍氏城（今阿富汗巴爾赫）· 駐節大月氏與大夏·考索諸國 Great Yuezhi & Daxia (Balkh, Afghanistan) — Court of the Great Yuezhi & Bactria
前128年（元朔元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

抵阿姆河流域，大月氏已臣服大夏（巴克特里亞），土地肥美安樂，無意東還復仇。張騫駐留歲餘，詳考大夏、安息、身毒及蜀布竹杖商路。

In fertile Bactria, the Yuezhi no longer wished to return east against the Xiongnu. Staying over a year, Zhang Qian documented Parthia, India, and Sichuan trade goods.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“留歲餘，還，並南山，欲從羌中歸。”Records of the Grand Historian: "After staying over a year, he headed back along the Southern Mountains to return through Qiang lands."
第 8 站 於闐·南道（今新疆和田）· 循崑崙南道東歸·復陷匈奴 Khotan (Yutian) (Hotan, Xinjiang) — Return via the Southern Tarim Route
前127年（元朔二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

為避匈奴控制的天山北道，返程改循崑崙山北麓西域南道，經莎車、於闐、樓蘭，欲從羌中歸漢，不意復為匈奴遊騎所得。

To avoid Xiongnu patrols on the northern route, Zhang Qian returned along the Kunlun foothills via Yarkand and Khotan, but was captured again by Xiongnu horsemen.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“並南山，欲從羌中歸，復為匈奴所得。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Skirting the Southern Mountains to return via Qiang territory, he was captured once more by the Xiongnu."
第 9 站 長安（歸漢）（今陝西西安）· 持節歸朝·絲路鑿空 Chang'an (Return) (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Return to Chang'an — Opening the Silk Road
前126年（元朔三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

元朔三年軍臣單於死、匈奴內亂，張騫與胡妻及甘父逃歸長安。出使十三載，百餘人唯二人得還，封博望侯，史稱“鑿空”。

Amid Xiongnu succession turmoil in 126 BCE, Zhang Qian escaped back to Chang'an with Ganfu—only two survivors of the original hundred—opening the Silk Road.

《史記·大宛列傳》：“初行時百餘人，去十三歲，唯二人得還。”Records of the Grand Historian: "Of over a hundred men who set out, after thirteen years only two returned."
