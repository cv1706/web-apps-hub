諸葛亮出山北伐行跡（207年 — 234年 · 東漢建安十二年至蜀漢建興十二年）

Zhuge Liang's Southern & Northern Campaigns (207 CE – 234 CE · Late Eastern Han to Three Kingdoms)

時代跨度：207年 — 234年 · 東漢建安十二年至蜀漢建興十二年 途經站點：共 10 站 起點 → 終點：隆中（襄陽） → 五丈原（岐山）

諸葛亮出山北伐行跡 · 逐站時空座標、古今地名與史料詩詞詳解（10 站）

第 1 站 隆中（襄陽）（今湖北襄陽）· 三顧茅廬·隆中決策 Longzhong (Xiangyang) (Xiangyang, Hubei) — Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage — The Longzhong Plan
207年（建安十二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

劉備駐新野，三詣襄陽城西二十里隆中草廬訪諸葛亮。年二十七的孔明縱論天下大勢，提出跨有荊益、保其巖阻、西和諸戎、南撫夷越、內修政理、待天下有變則命一上將將荊州之軍向宛洛而身率益州之眾出秦川的“隆中對”宏圖。

At age twenty-seven in his cottage at Longzhong west of Xiangyang, Zhuge Liang received Liu Bei on his third visit and unveiled the Longzhong Plan: secure Jing and Yi provinces, ally with Eastern Wu, pacify the southern tribes, and launch a two-pronged northern offensive to restore the Han.

諸葛亮《出師表》：“臣本布衣，躬耕於南陽，苟全性命於亂世，不求聞達於諸侯。先帝不以臣卑鄙，猥自枉屈，三顧臣於草廬之中。”Zhuge Liang, Former Chu Shi Biao: "I was originally a commoner, tilling the fields in Nanyang... Yet the Late Emperor did not disdain my humble station and deigned to visit me thrice in my thatched cottage."
第 2 站 柴桑·赤壁（今江西九江 / 湖北赤壁）· 奉使柴桑·聯吳破曹 Chaisang & Red Cliffs (Jiujiang, Jiangxi / Chibi, Hubei) — Diplomatic Mission to Chaisang & Victory at Red Cliffs
208年（建安十三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

曹操南下荊州、劉備敗走當陽長坂，諸葛亮臨危請命順江東下柴桑說孫權，剖析曹軍遠來疲弊、北人不習水戰之弊，促成孫劉同盟；旋於赤壁大破曹軍，奠定三分天下之基。

As Cao Cao swept south through Jing Province, Zhuge Liang sailed down the Yangtze to Chaisang to persuade Sun Quan into an alliance; the allied forces burned Cao Cao’s fleet at the Red Cliffs, forging the tripartite balance.

《三國志·諸葛亮傳》：“權大悅，即遣周瑜、程普、魯肅等水軍三萬，隨亮詣先主，併力拒曹公。”Records of the Three Kingdoms: "Sun Quan was greatly pleased and immediately dispatched Zhou Yu, Cheng Pu, and Lu Su with thirty thousand naval troops to join Liu Bei against Cao Cao."
第 3 站 成都（今四川成都）· 溯江入蜀·會師成都 Chengdu (Chengdu, Sichuan) — Ascending the Yangtze into Shu & Taking Chengdu
214年（建安十九年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

劉備入蜀受阻於雒城，諸葛亮與張飛、趙雲自荊州率軍溯長江西上，分定江州、江陽、犍為諸郡，與劉備合圍成都；劉璋出降，亮署左將軍府事，修訂《蜀科》，寬嚴相濟治理巴蜀。

Leading reinforcements up the Yangtze Three Gorges with Zhang Fei and Zhao Yun, Zhuge Liang converged on Chengdu with Liu Bei, securing Yi Province and codifying the legal code of Shu.

《三國志·諸葛亮傳》：“亮與張飛、趙雲等率眾泝江，分定郡縣，與先主共圍成都。成都平，以亮為軍師將軍。”Records of the Three Kingdoms: "Zhuge Liang, Zhang Fei, and Zhao Yun led forces up the Yangtze, pacifying commanderies and counties before joining Liu Bei to encircle Chengdu."
第 4 站 成都·武擔山（今四川成都）· 勸進昭烈·開府拜相 Chengdu (Capital of Shu Han) (Chengdu, Sichuan) — Founding of Shu Han & Appointment as Imperial Chancellor
221年（章武元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

曹丕篡漢自立，諸葛亮與群臣勸進劉備於成都武擔山之南即皇帝位，延續漢室正統；諸葛亮拜丞相、錄尚書事、假節，總攝蜀漢朝政與鹽鐵錦官經濟。

Following Cao Pi’s usurpation of the Han throne, Zhuge Liang advised Liu Bei to proclaim the continuation of Han in Chengdu, becoming Imperial Chancellor of Shu Han.

冊丞相詔：“朕遭家不造，奉承大統，兢兢業業，不敢康寧，思靖百姓，懼未能綏。於戲！丞相亮其悉朕意。”Imperial Edict of 221 CE: "Having inherited the Great Succession in troubled times, I rely wholeheartedly upon Chancellor Liang to bring peace to the realm."
第 5 站 永安（白帝城）（今重慶奉節）· 白帝託孤·受命危難 Yong'an (Baidicheng) (Fengjie, Chongqing) — Entrustment of the Orphan Emperor at Baidicheng
223年（章武三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

夷陵之戰後劉備病篤永安白帝城，自成都召諸葛亮赴行宮託孤，囑以“嗣子可輔，輔之；如其不才，君可自取”。亮涕泣死節，回朝後開府治事、遣鄧芝修好東吳，穩固危局。

Summoned to the dying Emperor Liu Bei’s bedside at Baidicheng, Zhuge Liang accepted the regency in tears, pledging unyielding loyalty until death and restoring the Wu alliance.

諸葛亮《出師表》：“受任於敗軍之際，奉命於危難之間，爾來二十有一年矣。”Zhuge Liang, Former Chu Shi Biao: "I received my appointment in the hour of defeat and accepted my mandate in the midst of peril—twenty-one years have passed since then."
第 6 站 滇池（南中）（今雲南昆明）· 渡瀘南征·七擒孟獲 Dianchi (Nanzhong) (Kunming, Yunnan) — Southern Campaign Across the Lu River — Pacifying Nanzhong
225年（建興三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

為解除北伐後顧之憂，諸葛亮率軍自安上水路入越巂，五月渡瀘水（金沙江），採馬謖“攻心為上”之策，七擒七縱孟獲，平定益州、永昌、牂牁、越巂四郡，皆即其渠帥而用之，終亮之世南方不復反。

Marching south across the Jinsha (Lu) River in summer 225 CE, Zhuge Liang adopted the strategy of "winning hearts above all," capturing and releasing Meng Huo seven times to secure the southern frontier.

《漢晉春秋》：“亮笑，縱使更戰，七縱七禽，而亮猶遣獲。獲止不去，曰：‘公，天威也，南人不復反矣。’”Annals of Han and Jin: "Captured and released seven times, Meng Huo bowed and refused to leave, saying: 'Your Excellency possesses the majesty of Heaven; the south will never rebel again.'"
第 7 站 漢中（沔陽）（今陝西漢中）· 進駐漢中·上出師表 Hanzhong (Mianyang) (Hanzhong, Shaanxi) — Garrisoning Hanzhong & Presenting the Chu Shi Biao
227年（建興五年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

南中既定，諸葛亮率諸軍北駐漢中沔陽，臨發向後主劉禪奏呈千古名篇《出師表》，勉以親賢臣遠小人、興復漢室還於舊都，以漢中為北伐大本營經略關隴。

Establishing his forward headquarters at Hanzhong, Zhuge Liang submitted his immortal Memorial on Deploying the Troops (Chu Shi Biao) to Emperor Liu Shan before marching north.

諸葛亮《出師表》：“今南方已定，兵甲已足，當獎率三軍，北定中原，庶竭駑鈍，攘除奸兇，興復漢室，還於舊都。”Zhuge Liang, Former Chu Shi Biao: "Now the south is pacified and our armor is sufficient; I ought to lead the Three Armies to secure the Central Plains and restore the House of Han."
第 8 站 祁山·天水（今甘肅禮縣 / 天水）· 一出祁山·收姜維失街亭 Mount Qi & Tianshui (Lixian / Tianshui, Gansu) — First Expedition from Mount Qi — Recruiting Jiang Wei
228年（建興六年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

聲東擊西由箕谷設疑兵、親率大軍攻祁山，戎陣整齊，南安、天水、安定三郡叛魏應亮，關中震響，收天水俊傑姜維；惜馬謖違亮節度於街亭依山不下城，為張郃絕水道所破，亮拔西縣千餘家還漢中並自貶三等。

Debouching from Mount Qi in disciplined array, Zhuge Liang won over three Wei commanderies—shaking all Guanzhong and recruiting Jiang Wei—before Ma Su’s defeat at Jieting forced an orderly withdrawal.

《三國志·諸葛亮傳》：“亮身率諸軍攻祁山，戎陣整齊，賞罰肅而號令明，南安、天水、安定三郡叛魏應亮，關中響震。”Records of the Three Kingdoms: "Zhuge Liang led the armies in person to attack Mount Qi in disciplined ranks; Nan'an, Tianshui, and Anding commanderies defected from Wei to join him, shaking all Guanzhong."
第 9 站 滷城·木門道（今甘肅禮縣 / 天水秦州）· 木牛流馬·木門道射殺張郃 Lucheng & Mumen Trail (Lixian / Tianshui, Gansu) — Fourth Expedition — Victory at Lucheng & Mumen Ambush
231年（建興九年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

始以木牛運糧四出祁山，於滷城大破司馬懿主力，獲甲首三千級、玄鎧五千領；夏六月糧盡退軍，司馬懿遣名將張郃追至木門道，亮乘高布伏萬弩齊發，射殺張郃。

Using wooden-ox transport on his fourth northern campaign, Zhuge Liang routed Sima Yi outside Lucheng and ambushed the pursuing Wei general Zhang He with massed crossbows in Mumen Gorge.

《三國志·諸葛亮傳》：“亮糧盡退軍，司馬宣王遣張郃追之，郃進至木門，與亮軍交戰，飛矢中郃右膝，薨。”Records of the Three Kingdoms: "When Zhuge Liang withdrew for lack of grain, Sima Yi sent Zhang He in pursuit; at Mumen Trail, a flying bolt struck Zhang He in the right knee, and he perished."
第 10 站 五丈原（岐山）（今陝西寶雞岐山）· 出斜谷屯五丈原·秋風星落 Wuzhang Plains (Qishan) (Qishan, Baoji, Shaanxi) — Final Stand on the Wuzhang Plains
234年（建興十二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

春二月悉大眾十萬由褒斜道出斜谷，以流馬運糧，據武功五丈原與司馬懿對峙於渭水之南，分兵屯田久駐之基，百姓安堵軍無私焉；相持百餘日，八月積勞病逝於軍中，年五十四，楊儀、姜維整軍從容退入斜谷，留“死諸葛走生仲達”之諺。

Leading 100,000 troops through the Xie Valley to the Wuzhang Plains south of the Wei River, Zhuge Liang established military-agricultural colonies and held off Sima Yi for over a hundred days before passing away in camp at age fifty-four.

杜甫《蜀相》：“三顧頻煩天下計，兩朝開濟老臣心。出師未捷身先死，長使英雄淚滿襟。”Du Fu, The Chancellor of Shu: "Three visits sought his counsel for the realm; through two reigns the old minister’s heart sustained the state. Before the campaign triumphed he died in the field, forever filling heroes’ robes with tears."
