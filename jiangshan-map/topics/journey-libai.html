李白仗劍壯遊行跡（701年 — 762年 · 武周長安元年至唐寶應元年）

Li Bai's Grand Travels Across Tang China (701 CE – 762 CE · High Tang Dynasty)

李白仗劍壯遊行跡 · 逐站時空座標、古今地名與史料詩詞詳解（10 站）

第 1 站 碎葉城（今吉爾吉斯斯坦托克馬克）· 誕於西域·安西碎葉 Suyab (Anxi Protectorate) (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) — Birth at Suyab in the Western Regions
701年（長安元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

長安元年（701年），李白誕於唐安西都護府碎葉城（今楚河流域托克馬克附近），幼承絲路胡漢交融之風，五歲隨父李客東遷入蜀。

Born in 701 CE at Suyab—one of the Four Garrisons of Anxi in the Chu River valley—Li Bai spent his earliest childhood on the Silk Road frontier before migrating with his family to Sichuan at age five.

範傳正《唐左拾遺翰林學士李公新墓碑》：“隋末多難，一房被竄於碎葉，流離散落，隱易姓名。”Fan Chuanzheng, New Tomb Inscription for Li Bai: "Amid the turmoil at the end of Sui, his branch of the clan was exiled to Suyab, living incognito until returning to Shu in the Shenlong era."
第 2 站 綿州昌隆（青蓮）（今四川江油青蓮鎮）· 卜居青蓮·大匡山讀書 Qinglian, Mianzhou (Jiangyou, Sichuan) — Youth in Qinglian & Studying on Mount Dakuang
705年（神龍元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

隨父客居劍南道綿州昌隆縣青蓮鄉（自號“青蓮居士”）。少時博學廣覽、好劍術任俠，隱大匡山讀書，後遊成都、登峨眉，受道士司馬承禎贊為“有仙風道骨，可與神遊八極之表”，作《大鵬遇希有鳥賦》。

Settling in Qinglian in Mianzhou (whence his sobriquet "Layman of Blue Lotus"), young Li Bai practiced swordsmanship, studied on Mount Dakuang, climbed Mount Emei, and was praised by Taoist master Sima Chengzhen as having "immortal bones."

李白《上安州裴長史書》：“五歲誦六甲，十歲觀百家……十五觀奇書，作賦凌相如。”Li Bai, Letter to Administrator Pei of Anzhou: "At five I recited the calendar classics; at ten I read the Hundred Schools; at fifteen I composed rhapsodies rivaling Sima Xiangru."
第 3 站 渝州·荊門（今重慶 / 湖北宜都）· 仗劍去國·辭親出三峽 Yuzhou & Jingmen Gorge (Chongqing / Yidu, Hubei) — Leaving Shu with Sword in Hand Through the Three Gorges
724年（開元十二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

年二十四的李白懷濟世遠志“仗劍去國，辭親遠遊”，自成都乘舟順岷江下渝州，穿巫峽、出荊門，初見江漢平原天地壯闊，寫下《峨眉山月歌》與《渡荊門送別》。

At twenty-four, Li Bai bade farewell to Sichuan and sailed down the Three Gorges past Jingmen into the vast Jianghan Plain, beginning his lifelong travels across the Tang Empire.

李白《渡荊門送別》：“山隨平野盡，江入大荒流。月下飛天鏡，雲生結海樓。”Li Bai, Farewell at Jingmen: "Mountains vanish into the level plain; the Great River rolls into the boundless wild. Beneath the moon a celestial mirror flies; rising clouds weave ocean pavilions."
第 4 站 金陵·揚州（今江蘇南京 / 揚州）· 漫遊吳越·散金三十萬 Jinling & Yangzhou (Nanjing / Yangzhou, Jiangsu) — Grand Tour of Wu-Yue & Spending 300,000 Cash in Yangzhou
726年（開元十四年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

順江東下，登廬山望瀑布、遊金陵懷古，轉赴繁華甲天下的淮南都會揚州與吳越諸郡，豪爽濟困，“不逾一年，散金三十餘萬”，寫就《望廬山瀑布》《靜夜思》等千古絕唱。

Sailing down the lower Yangtze past Mount Lu and Jinling to prosperous Yangzhou, Li Bai generously aided destitute scholars—giving away over 300,000 coins within a year—and composed Quiet Night Thought.

李白《上安州裴長史書》：“曩昔東遊維揚，不逾一年，散金三十餘萬，有落魄公子，悉皆濟之。”Li Bai: "Formerly traveling east to Yangzhou, in less than a year I scattered more than three hundred thousand in gold, coming to the aid of every stranded gentleman."
第 5 站 安陸·江夏（今湖北安陸 / 武漢）· 酒隱安陸·登臨黃鶴樓 Anlu & Jiangxia (Yellow Crane Tower) (Anlu / Wuhan, Hubei) — Ten Years in Anlu & Farewell at Yellow Crane Tower
727年（開元十五年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

西歸荊楚，贅於安陸故相許圉師孫女家，隱居白兆山桃花巖所謂“酒隱安陸，蹉跎十年”；其間頻遊江夏與襄陽，結交孟浩然，於黃鶴樓賦詩送其東下廣陵。

Marrying into the family of former Chancellor Xu Yushi in Anlu, Li Bai made Mount Baizhao his home base for a decade, befriending Meng Haoran and seeing him off at the Yellow Crane Tower.

李白《黃鶴樓送孟浩然之廣陵》：“故人西辭黃鶴樓，煙花三月下揚州。孤帆遠影碧空盡，唯見長江天際流。”Li Bai, Seeing Off Meng Haoran at Yellow Crane Tower: "My old friend bids farewell to the west at Yellow Crane Tower, sailing down to Yangzhou in the misty blossoms of March."
第 6 站 長安（今陝西西安）· 奉詔入京·翰林待詔 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Summoned to Chang'an — Imperial Hanlin Academy
742年（天寶元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

因道士吳筠及玉真公主舉薦，唐玄宗降詔徵召入京。李白高歌“仰天大笑出門去，我輩豈是蓬蒿人”西入長安，賀知章讀《蜀道難》驚呼“謫仙人”，玄宗降輦步迎、供奉翰林，賦《清平調》三章。

Summoned to Chang’an by Emperor Xuanzong, Li Bai was hailed by He Zhizhang as a "Banished Immortal" upon reading The Hard Roads of Shu, and served in the Imperial Hanlin Academy.

李白《南陵別兒童入京》：“仰天大笑出門去，我輩豈是蓬蒿人！”Li Bai, Parting from My Children at Nanling for the Capital: "Throwing back my head with a great laugh I stride out the gate—how could men like us remain forever amid the mugwort?"
第 7 站 洛陽·梁宋（今河南洛陽 / 商丘開封）· 賜金放還·洛陽逢杜甫 Luoyang & Liang-Song (Luoyang / Kaifeng, Henan) — Leaving Court & Historic Meeting with Du Fu in Luoyang
744年（天寶三載） · 在地圖定位此站 →

因傲岸不羈遭權貴讒毀，玄宗“賜金放還”。李白東出長安至東都洛陽，與年三十三的杜甫初遇定交，復邀高適同遊梁宋（開封、商丘），登吹臺、獵孟諸，淋漓盡致展現盛唐雙子星風採。

Granted gold to leave court in 744 CE, Li Bai traveled east to Luoyang where he met the younger Du Fu—a meeting hailed as the twin peaks of Tang poetry—and roamed the Liang-Song plains with Gao Shi.

杜甫《寄李十二白二十韻》：“乞歸優詔許，遇我宿心親。醉舞梁園夜，行歌泗水春。”Du Fu, Twenty Rhymes Sent to Li Bai: "Your plea to retire was granted by gracious edict; meeting me, our kindred hearts grew close—dancing drunk in Liang Garden by night, singing along the Si River in spring."
第 8 站 齊魯（兗州任城）（今山東濟寧）· 東遊齊魯·夢遊天姥 Qilu (Rencheng, Yanzhou) (Jining, Shandong) — Sojourn in Qilu & Dreaming of Mount Tianmu
745年（天寶四載） · 在地圖定位此站 →

寄家山東兗州任城，與杜甫攜手同遊齊魯，訪範十隱居、登泰山徂徠，於紫極宮受道籙；臨別東魯南遊越中時賦《夢遊天姥吟留別》，喊出“安能摧眉折腰事權貴，使我不得開心顏”之千古強音。

Making his family home in Rencheng (Shandong) and roaming Qilu with Du Fu, Li Bai composed Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: A Farewell Song before heading south.

李白《夢遊天姥吟留別》：“安能摧眉折腰事權貴，使我不得開心顏！”Li Bai, Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: "How can I bow my brow and bend my waist to serve the powerful, denying myself a joyful heart and open brow?"
第 9 站 白帝城（夔州）（今重慶奉節）· 長流夜郎·白帝遇赦還江陵 Baidicheng (Kuizhou) (Fengjie, Chongqing) — Exile to Yelang & Imperial Pardon at Baidicheng
759年（乾元二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

安史之亂起，李白懷平胡復國之志入永王李璘幕府，兵敗後系潯陽獄，坐長流夜郎（黔中）；乾元二年春溯江行至巫山白帝城，忽逢關內大旱朝廷大赦天下，驚喜放舟東下江陵，寫下《早發白帝城》。

Implicated in Prince Lin of Yong’s campaign during the An Lushan Rebellion, Li Bai was banished toward distant Yelang; reaching Baidicheng in spring 759 CE, he received an imperial amnesty and raced downstream to Jiangling.

李白《早發白帝城》：“朝辭白帝彩雲間，千里江陵一日還。兩岸猿聲啼不住，輕舟已過萬重山。”Li Bai, Departing Baidicheng at Dawn: "At dawn I leave Baidicheng amid rainbow clouds; a thousand li to Jiangling I return in a single day. While gibbons on both banks cry without cease, my light skiff has already shot past ten thousand mountains."
第 10 站 當塗（宣城郡）（今安徽馬鞍山·當塗）· 賦臨路歌·終老當塗青山 Dangtu (Xuancheng) (Dangtu, Ma'anshan, Anhui) — Final Song at Dangtu & Resting by Mount Qing
762年（寶應元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

晚年漂泊金陵、宣城、秋浦之間，聞太尉李光弼統軍討史朝義，六十一歲仍請纓從軍，行至金陵因病折返，依族叔當塗令李陽冰；寶應元年十一月賦《臨路歌》絕筆，病逝於當塗，終葬謝朓曾遊之青山。

After attempting at sixty-one to join Li Guangbi’s army against the rebels, an ailing Li Bai stayed with his kinsman Li Yangbing in Dangtu, composing his final Song Before the Road and passing away in late 762 CE.

李白《臨路歌》：“大鵬飛兮振八裔，中天摧兮力不濟。餘風激兮萬世，遊扶桑兮掛石袂。”Li Bai, Song Before the Road: "The Great Peng soared and shook the eight horizons; broken in mid-heaven, its strength gave out—yet its lingering wind stirs ten thousand generations."
