王陽明知行悟道行跡（1472年 — 1529年 · 明成化八年至嘉靖七年）

Wang Yangming's Unity of Knowledge & Action (1472 CE – 1529 CE · Ming Dynasty)

時代跨度：1472年 — 1529年 · 明成化八年至嘉靖七年 途經站點：共 10 站 起點 → 終點：餘姚 → 南安

全線途經古今地名：餘姚（今浙江寧波餘姚） → 廣信（今江西上饒） → 京師（今北京） → 錢塘江（今浙江杭州錢塘江口） → 龍場（今貴州貴陽修文） → 廬陵（今江西吉安） → 贛州（今江西贛州） → 南昌（今江西南昌·鄱陽湖） → 紹興（今浙江紹興） → 南安（今江西贛州大余）。

王陽明知行悟道行跡 · 逐站時空座標、古今地名與史料詩詞詳解（10 站）

第 1 站 餘姚（今浙江寧波餘姚）· 降生瑞雲·志在聖賢 Yuyao (Yuyao, Ningbo, Zhejiang) — Birth at Ruiyun Tower & Aspiration for Sagehood
1472年（成化八年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

王守仁誕生於浙江紹興府餘姚縣瑞雲樓，父王華次年高中狀元。十一歲隨祖父北上京師塾讀，問塾師「何為第一等事」，師答「惟讀書登第耳」，守仁昂然對曰：「登第恐未為第一等事，或讀書學聖賢耳。」自幼立下成聖之志。

Born at Ruiyun Tower in Yuyao, Wang Shouren asked his tutor at age eleven what the highest pursuit in life was. Told it was passing the imperial exams, he replied: "Passing exams is surely not the highest pursuit; learning to become a sage is."

王陽明少時語：“登第恐未為第一等事，或讀書學聖賢耳。”——《王陽明先生年譜》Young Wang Yangming: "Passing the imperial examination is hardly the highest undertaking; reading to become a sage is."
第 2 站 廣信（今江西上饒）· 謁婁一齋·始聞格物 Guangxin (Shangrao, Jiangxi) — Meeting Lou Liang at Guangxin & Inquiring into Gewu
1489年（弘治二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

十八歲自南昌迎婦歸餘姚，舟次江西廣信（今上饒），特往拜謁崇仁學派大儒婁諒（婁一齋）。婁諒向其闡發宋儒格物窮理之旨，語以「聖人必可學而至」，守仁深相契合，自此篤志聖學，歸後遍讀考亭朱子遺書。

Returning from Nanchang at age eighteen, Wang visited Neo-Confucian master Lou Liang at Guangxin, who assured him that sagehood could truly be attained through learning and inquiry.

《明史·王守仁傳》：“過廣信，謁婁諒，語以聖人可學而至，遂深契之。”History of Ming: "Passing through Guangxin, he visited Lou Liang, who taught that sagehood can be learned, striking a deep chord in him."
第 3 站 京師（今北京）· 格竹致疾·進士及第 Jingshi (Beijing) (Beijing) — Investigating Bamboo & Passing the Metropolitan Exam
1492年（弘治五年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

在京師欲依朱熹「即物窮理」之法窮究草木之理，與錢友同坐亭前面竹「格竹」七日七夜，理未得而勞神成疾，始疑外求之弊。弘治十二年（1499）賜進士及第，歷任工部、刑部主事，究心兵法韜略與經世之學。

In Beijing, Wang meditated before bamboo for seven days trying to grasp its Neo-Confucian principle, falling ill and doubting externalism. In 1499 he passed the palace exam and served in the Ministries of Works and Justice.

《傳習錄》：“眾人只說格物要依晦翁，何曾把他的說去用？我著實曾用來。”Instructions for Practical Living: "People say we must follow Zhu Xi in investigating things, yet who has truly practiced it? I genuinely tried for seven days."
第 4 站 錢塘江（今浙江杭州錢塘江口）· 廷杖下獄·投江脫險 Qiantang River (Hangzhou, Zhejiang) — Flogged in Court & Escaping Assassins at Qiantang
1506年（正德元年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

正德元年，司禮監權宦劉瑾擅權，逮捕南京給事中戴銑等二十餘人。守仁抗疏力救，觸怒劉瑾，被廷杖四十下詔獄，謫貴州龍場驛丞。南下途經杭州錢塘江，劉瑾遣刺客尾隨欲加害，守仁夜擲衣冠於江中留絕命詩詐死，乘海舟脫險。

For defending upright officials against eunuch dictator Liu Jin, Wang was flogged forty strokes, imprisoned, and banished to Guizhou. Pursued by Liu Jin’s assassins at Qiantang River, he faked his drowning at night to escape.

王陽明《泛海》：“險夷原不滯胸中，何異浮雲過太空！夜靜海濤三萬裡，月明飛錫下天風。”Wang Yangming, Sailing the Sea: "Danger and safety leave no trace in my breast, like floating clouds across the vast sky!"
第 5 站 龍場（今貴州貴陽修文）· 龍場大悟·創立心學 Longchang (Xiuwen, Guiyang, Guizhou) — Longchang Enlightenment & Philosophy of Mind
1508年（正德三年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

抵達貴州萬山叢棘、瘴癘橫行之龍場驛。居東洞石棺之中，日夜端居澄默，自問「聖人處此更有何道」。正德三年中夜忽然大悟格物致知之旨，徹見「聖人之道，吾性自足，向之求理於事物者誤也」，繼而倡「知行合一」，開五百年陽明心學。

Living in a stone cave in remote Guizhou, Wang meditated day and night on life and death. One midnight in 1508 he achieved sudden enlightenment: "The Way of the Sages is complete within my own nature; seeking principles in external things was mistaken."

《王陽明年譜》：“聖人之道，吾性自足，向之求理於事物者誤也。”Chronicle of Wang Yangming: "The Way of the Sages is self-sufficient within our own mind-nature; seeking principle outside in external things was an error."
第 6 站 廬陵（今江西吉安）· 知縣廬陵·興教善政 Luling (Ji'an, Jiangxi) — Magistrate of Luling — Benevolent Governance
1510年（正德五年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

劉瑾伏誅前夕，守仁升任江西吉安府廬陵縣知縣。視事七月，不以威刑而重告諭，均徭役、息詞訟、立十家牌保甲法，選里老敦厚者勸誡鄉民，廬陵風俗翕然丕變，為日後經略江西奠定深厚民心。

Appointed magistrate of Luling County in Jiangxi, Wang governed through moral persuasion rather than harsh punishment, equalizing corvée labor and establishing community compacts.

王陽明《告諭廬陵父老》：“一念之善，即吉神佑之；一念之惡，即厲鬼隨之。”Proclamation to the Elders of Luling: "A single good thought brings auspicious blessings; a single evil thought invites calamity."
第 7 站 贛州（今江西贛州）· 巡撫南贛·破山中賊 Ganzhou (Ganzhou, Jiangxi) — Governor of Southern Gan — Quelling Mountain Bandits
1517年（正德十二年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

受兵部尚書王瓊力薦，以左僉都御史巡撫南贛汀漳。自編選練民兵，嚴明賞罰，兩年內連破詹師富、大帽山、橫水、桶岡、浰頭諸巢，蕩平盤踞贛閩粵湘邊界數十年之巨寇；繼而頒《南贛鄉約》、設崇義縣，興立社學教化百姓。

As Governor of Southern Gan, Wang trained local militias and eradicated decades-old bandit strongholds across the Jiangxi–Fujian–Guangdong border, then instituted the Southern Gan Community Compact.

王陽明《與楊仕德薛尚謙書》：“破山中賊易，破心中賊難。”Letter to Yang Shide: "It is easy to vanquish the bandits in the mountains, but hard to vanquish the bandits in the mind."
第 8 站 南昌（今江西南昌·鄱陽湖）· 平寧王亂·奇兵定難 Nanchang (Nanchang & Poyang Lake, Jiangxi) — Crushing Prince of Ning's Rebellion in 35 Days
1519年（正德十四年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

六月，寧王朱宸濠於南昌起兵十萬反叛，順江東下圍安慶、逼南京。守仁在豐城聞變，退保吉安特徵集義軍，施反間疑兵計遲滯叛軍，隨即乘虛直搗南昌老巢。寧王回救，兩軍決戰於鄱陽湖黃家渡，守仁火攻大破叛軍，僅三十五日生擒朱宸濠。

When Prince Zhu Chenhao rebelled with 100,000 troops, Wang rallied local forces in Ji’an, used deception to stall the rebels, stormed their base at Nanchang, and captured the prince after a fire-ship battle on Poyang Lake in just 35 days.

王陽明《擒獲宸濠捷音疏》：“臣等仰仗天威，一鼓而克其巢穴，再戰而擒其渠魁。”Victory Memorial on Capturing Chenhao: "With one drumbeat we stormed his lair; in the second battle we captured the rebel chief alive."
第 9 站 紹興（今浙江紹興）· 致良知教·天泉證道 Shaoxing (Shaoxing, Zhejiang) — Doctrine of Liangzhi & Tianquan Bridge Discourse
1521年（正德十六年） · 在地圖定位此站 →

明世宗即位，封守仁新建伯，守仁歸越城紹興，於稽山書院、陽明洞聚徒講學，正式揭出「致良知」三字教法，四方學者雲集。嘉靖六年（1527）奉詔出征兩廣前夕，於天泉橋與大弟子錢德洪、王畿印證心學「四句教」，成心學傳世綱領。

Enfeoffed as Earl of Xinjian, Wang lectured at Shaoxing and proclaimed the doctrine of "Extending Innate Moral Knowledge" (Zhi Liangzhi). On the eve of his Guangxi campaign in 1527, he delivered the Four-Sentence Teaching at Tianquan Bridge.

《天泉證道·四句教》：“無善無噁心之體，有善有惡意之動。知善知惡是良知，為善去惡是格物。”Four-Sentence Teaching: "Neither good nor evil is the substance of the mind; good and evil arise with the movement of intention. Knowing good and evil is innate knowledge; doing good and removing evil is the investigation of things."
第 10 站 南安（今江西贛州大余）· 此心光明·亦復何言 Nan'an (Dayu, Ganzhou, Jiangxi) — Radiant Mind — Final Passing at Qinglongpu
1529年（嘉靖七年冬） · 在地圖定位此站 →

嘉靖六年總督兩廣，不費一兵一卒招撫思恩、田州盧蘇王受七萬眾，繼以奇兵蕩平斷藤峽八寨瑤亂。次年冬肺疾加劇上疏乞歸，十一月二十九日（公曆1529年1月）舟次江西南安府大余縣青龍鋪。門人周積問遺言，先生微笑曰：「此心光明，亦復何言！」遂瞑目而逝。

After pacifying Guangxi’s Si’en, Tianzhou, and Rattan Gorge rebellions, Wang fell gravely ill on his journey home. Passing away on a boat at Qinglongpu in Nan’an, he spoke his final words: "This mind is luminous; what more is there to say?"

王陽明臨終遺言：“此心光明，亦復何言！”——《王陽明先生年譜》Wang Yangming’s last words: "This mind is luminous—what more is there to say!"
